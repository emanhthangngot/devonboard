# Case Study: DORA Research On AI-Assisted Software Delivery

## 1. Problem Statement

AI coding tools can increase individual development speed, but software delivery depends on more than code generation. Teams still need review quality, testing, context, and operational discipline. Without those foundations, faster code output can increase instability.

## 2. Target Personas & User Research

*   **Primary persona:** Engineering leaders and platform teams adopting AI development tools.
*   **Secondary persona:** Developers and reviewers working with larger AI-assisted changesets.

**Key Pain Points Solved:**

*   Faster code generation can outpace review and QA.
*   Teams need better context to verify generated or proposed changes.
*   AI tooling must support delivery reliability, not just local productivity.

## 3. The Solution

DORA's research frames AI as an amplifier of existing engineering systems. Teams with strong practices benefit more; teams with weak review/testing/context processes can amplify risk.

### The Mechanisms

1.  Measure delivery performance and reliability.
2.  Identify organizational and workflow constraints.
3.  Treat AI adoption as a system-level change, not a tool-only change.

## 4. Impact and Success Metrics

*   **Delivery performance:** Deployment frequency, lead time, change failure rate, recovery time.
*   **Developer experience:** Flow, productivity, satisfaction.
*   **Review burden:** Whether faster code creation creates downstream verification bottlenecks.

## 5. Lifecycle of the Feature & Lessons Learnt

*   **How it started:** DORA research measured DevOps capabilities before the AI coding wave.
*   **Key insight / pivot:** AI improves outcomes only when the surrounding engineering system can absorb faster output.
*   **Lesson for us:** DevOnboard should reduce verification burden by making code history and rationale easy to inspect.
